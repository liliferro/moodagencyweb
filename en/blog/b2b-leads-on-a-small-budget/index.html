How to generate B2B leads on a small budget

Five ways to generate B2B opportunities with limited resources: open quotes, referrals, prospecting, your website and content.

By Mood Agency · Published on November 14, 2026

With a small budget, one rule comes first: before paying to attract new people, make the most of what you already have. Reviewing existing customers, open quotes and companies that fit your offer can be a useful first step before increasing investment. None of these actions guarantees results.

This is a practical sequence for an initial pilot. Adjust it to your sales cycle and resources; your team's time also has a cost.

1. Revisit quotes that didn't close

They've already shown interest. Review quotes from the past six months and follow up using the 7-touch sequence.

2. Ask for referrals systematically

Don't wait for referrals to happen. Ask each satisfied customer for two specific introductions: one of their suppliers and a colleague at another company. Offer to draft the introduction yourself.

3. Build a short list and reach out individually

Start with a small list you can research and serve well. Find companies in a public directory (here's how to use DENUE) and write personalized messages with AI's help (20 prompts).

4. Fix your website before sending it traffic

If your website doesn't convert, visits go to waste. Review why your website isn't generating quote requests.

5. Publish answers to your customers' questions

Write down the ten questions you hear most often in sales and answer them on your website, one per page. Content can remain useful over time, but it needs updating and doesn't guarantee traffic.

What can wait

Hold off on ads until your website can convert visitors and you have a process to handle leads. Paying for visits nobody follows up on quickly burns through a small budget.

Try this today

  • Count your open quotes from the last six months
  • Choose five customers to ask for referrals this week
  • List your customers' ten most common questions

Frequently asked questions

How much time does it take?

Set aside a weekly block and measure the time spent researching, contacting and responding. Compare opportunities and total cost, not just ad spend.

Can LinkedIn help without paid ads?

Yes, for connecting with people on your list and sharing your industry knowledge.
