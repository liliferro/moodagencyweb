How to find companies that need your product in Mexico

A four-step method to build a prospect list in Mexico using INEGI's DENUE directory and AI.

By Mood Agency · Published on November 14, 2026

To finding companies that need your product, you don't have to buy a database. INEGI publishes a free directory of over 6 million establishments, filterable by industry, size and location. AI can then help you organize the list.

This method helps you build an initial, verifiable list, step by step.

Step 1: describe your best customer with three details

Think of your five biggest customers and note what they have in common:

  • Industry: what they do.
  • Size: how many employees they have.
  • Location: where they operate.

Example: food manufacturers with more than 50 employees in Mexico's Bajío region.

Step 2: search DENUE

The National Statistical Directory of Economic Units is publicly available. Its May 2026 edition lists 6,138,075 establishments (INEGI).

Open the directory and select the economic activity, establishment size and state or municipality. The result is a list of establishments matching that profile—not evidence that they need your product or want to buy. Deduplicate branches if you're analyzing companies rather than locations, and verify each record.

Step 3: use AI to organize, not invent

Get the list from the directory, not the assistant. If you ask an AI assistant for 'food manufacturers in the Bajío,' the list may be incomplete or include businesses that no longer exist.

What AI can do well is analyze a list you provide:

1. Review each company's website and summarize what it does.

2. Flag those that most closely resemble your best customers.

3. Suggest why they might need your product.

We have 20 prompts ready for this task.

Step 4: find the right person

The directory describes the business, not the decision-maker. Research who is involved in purchasing: procurement, operations or leadership, depending on the business. Don't assume a job title proves decision-making authority. When reaching out, identify yourself, explain why you're contacting them and offer a way to opt out.

Try this today

  • Write down your ideal customer's industry, size and location
  • Count how many companies match that profile in DENUE
  • Compare that number with how many your team already knows

Frequently asked questions

Does DENUE cost anything?

No. It's a public tool provided by INEGI.

Does it include emails and phone numbers?

It includes establishment details. Contact information isn't always complete or current and needs verification.

How often is it updated?

INEGI publishes periodic editions. The latest at the time this article was written was May 2026.
