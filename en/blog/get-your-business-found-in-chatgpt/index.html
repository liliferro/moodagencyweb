How to show up when people ask ChatGPT about your product

Steps to make your business information accessible and verifiable in ChatGPT Search, with crawling controls and measurement—without guarantees.

By Mood Agency · Published on November 14, 2026

In this guide
  • Start with a simple test
  • Six steps to strengthen your visibility
  • What to expect
  • Try this today
  • Frequently asked questions

To improve your business's visibility in AI answers, publish accessible, clear and verifiable information. Absence from an answer doesn't establish a specific cause: the question, context, sources and product all play a role.

The goal is to help a buyer understand and verify your offer. No optimization guarantees an assistant will recommend you.

Start with a simple test

Open two assistants and ask the question your customer would ask: 'Who sells [your product] in [your area]?' Record who appears, what the answers say about each company and which sources they cite. That's your baseline.

Six steps to strengthen your visibility

1. Make your website readable. Check that your priority pages have accessible text and links. PDFs can be crawled, but a browsable catalog is a useful complement. For ChatGPT Search, check OAI-SearchBot access and server blocks; GPTBot's training controls are separate. Use our technical SEO guide.

2. Be specific about what you do. 'We manufacture rubber seals for the food industry in Querétaro' gives an assistant useful information. 'Industrial solutions' doesn't.

3. Answer real questions. Create a page for each common customer question, with the answer in the opening lines. See how in useful content for people, search engines and AI answers.

4. Publish evidence. Share case studies with numbers, clients and certifications.

5. Earn mentions elsewhere. Look at industry directories, trade associations, trade publications and reviews. Legitimate references help buyers verify your information; they don't guarantee AI citations.

6. Use a consistent name everywhere. Keep your business name, city and description consistent across your website, LinkedIn and directories.

What to expect

There is no way to guarantee an assistant will recommend you, and answers vary from one query to another. What you can do is repeat the same questions each month and look for trends.

Try this today

  • Test two assistants and save their answers
  • Rewrite your homepage's opening paragraph to explain what you do, who you serve and where
  • List five industry directories or publications where your business should appear

Frequently asked questions

Does this replace SEO?

No. It complements it: Google applies SEO fundamentals to its own AI features. That doesn't establish that a specific Google ranking determines another provider's recommendations.

Can you pay to guarantee an organic recommendation?

No. Paid advertising and organic mentions are different things. Don't buy promises of guaranteed recommendations.

How long does it take?

There is no guaranteed timeline. Record when you publish each change and repeat a consistent sample of questions. Don't treat a single answer as a definitive result.
