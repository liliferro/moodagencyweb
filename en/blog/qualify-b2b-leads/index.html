How to qualify B2B leads with AI

Four criteria to assess a lead, a ready-to-use scoring table and ways AI can help you apply it.

By Mood Agency · Published on November 14, 2026

In this guide
  • The four criteria
  • A simple scoring table
  • Where AI helps
  • Try this today
  • Frequently asked questions

Qualifying a lead means answering one question: is this prospect worth a salesperson's time today? Use four criteria agreed on by marketing and sales. Without that agreement, marketing delivers contacts that sales dismisses as unusable.

The four criteria

CriterionQuestionWhat to look for
FitIs this the kind of business we sell to?Industry, size and location
NeedDo they have a problem we solve?What they said in the form or on the call
Decision-makingAre we speaking to a decision-maker or an influencer?Their role
TimingDo they need it soon?The timeline they mentioned

A simple scoring table

Criterion0 points1 point2 points
FitOutside our target profilePartial matchIdeal fit
NeedUnclearGeneralSpecific
Decision-makingNot involved in the purchaseInfluences the decisionMakes the decision
TimingNo timelineThis yearWithin 90 days

Example prioritization: 6–8 points, prioritize sales review; 3–5 points, clarify questions and agree on follow-up; 0–2 points, check for missing data before ruling out the lead. Don't confuse unknown information with a poor fit.

Adjust the thresholds to your business. What matters is that sales and marketing use the same table.

Where AI helps

  • Read the lead's message and suggest a score with an explanation.
  • Extract industry and company size from available sources, citing the source and date. Mark unavailable information as unknown.
  • Summarize the conversation for the salesperson before the call.

A person makes the final decision. A conversational landing page collects these four details before the first contact.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between a contact and a qualified lead?

A contact has shared their details. A qualified lead meets the agreed criteria.

Should I discard low-scoring leads?

Not automatically. First distinguish missing data, a future need and a poor fit. Record the reason and review the decision with sales.

How do I know whether it's working?

Compare how many qualified leads turn into quotes. We explain this in how to measure leads without confusing clicks with customers.

Examples and templates are for guidance. Adapt the criteria to your operations and verify information before using it commercially.
