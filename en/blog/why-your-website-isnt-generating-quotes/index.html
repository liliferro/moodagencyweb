Why your website isn't generating quote requests

Five reasons an industrial website isn't generating quote requests, and what to change in each case—starting today.

By Mood Agency · Published on November 14, 2026

If your website gets visits but no quote requests, review traffic quality, the clarity of your offer and how easy it is to contact you. Visit counts alone won't identify the problem. Reviewing inquiries, pages and forms helps you decide what to change.

Here are five issues worth checking on industrial business websites, and what to do about each.

1. It doesn't say what you sell or who it's for

Phrases like 'comprehensive quality solutions' tell buyers very little. Replace them with a specific headline: what you manufacture or distribute, for which industry and where you deliver.

2. It's a catalog, not a sales tool

A downloadable PDF doesn't answer questions. Each product or product line needs its own page with applications, specifications, delivery times and a quote request button.

3. There's no evidence

Industrial buyers want to know who you've worked with. Show customers, case studies with figures, certifications and real photos of your plant or warehouse. See how we present our case studies.

4. Getting in touch is hard

Review your form: does it ask for more than four details? Are phone and WhatsApp options visible? Does it work well on mobile? Each field should have a clear purpose. Check where users drop off before attributing lost inquiries to it. In a website that makes contact easy we explain how to review it.

5. Nobody responds promptly

A slow response can cool a buyer's interest. Define who responds and how quickly, and automate the initial follow-up.

Try this today

  • Ask someone outside your company to look at your homepage for ten seconds and tell you what you sell
  • Complete your own form on a phone
  • Count the clicks between a product and the quote request button

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a new website?

Not necessarily. First assess what can be improved on the current site.

How do I know whether the problem is the traffic or the website?

Review traffic intent, the contact journey and possible measurement errors. For reliable measurement, read how to measure leads without confusing clicks with customers.

Would an AI chat help?

It may help if it offers guidance based on validated information, provides access to a person and improves the journey. That needs testing. We explain it in conversational landing page.
