How to write cold emails with AI that don't sound AI-generated

A four-line structure for your first B2B email, how to prompt AI and the mistakes that make a message sound automated.

By Mood Agency · Published on November 14, 2026

A first B2B email is more relevant when it explains why you're contacting that specific business. AI can draft it in seconds, but only if you provide a real detail about the company. Without context, the draft may be generic. Personalization doesn't guarantee a reply, either.

The four-line structure

  1. Why them: a specific detail about their business.
  2. The problem you solve: one sentence, in their words.
  3. Evidence: a similar customer or a result.
  4. One easy-to-answer question.

Hypothetical example to adapt. Replace the placeholders and verify every claim before using it:

Subject: Seals for your new production line

Hi Laura, I saw you've opened a second production line in Celaya. We manufacture rubber seals for food-processing equipment and deliver across the Bajío within [timeframe]. We work with [customer in the same industry]. Who handles replacement parts at your plant?

Fewer than 60 words, no empty adjectives and a question that takes one line to answer.

How to prompt AI

'Write an email under 80 words to [role] at [company]. Company detail: [fact]. I sell: [product]. Similar customer: [customer]. Use a direct, respectful tone, no adjectives and no “I hope you're well.” End with a simple question.'

Find more prompts like this in 20 prompts for prospecting.

What makes an email sound automated

  • It opens with 'I hope this email finds you well.'
  • It talks about you before the reader.
  • It uses words such as 'innovative,' 'leading' or 'comprehensive solution.'
  • It asks for a 30-minute meeting in the first message.
  • It could be sent unchanged to any company.

Before you send

Read it aloud. If you wouldn't say it that way on the phone, change it. Include your name, your company and an easy way to ask you to stop emailing.

Try this today

  • Choose five companies and find one real detail about each
  • Request one draft per company using the prompt above
  • Trim each email to fewer than 80 words
