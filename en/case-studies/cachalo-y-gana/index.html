Cachalo y Gana

A live challenge: connecting mass audiences through speed and gamification.

Background

Cachalo y Gana was a live game show with real-time audience participation. The project needed a robust digital hub connecting the live broadcast with thousands of users joining simultaneously to take part.

The challenge

Our challenge was to build a WordPress platform that was easy to manage and could handle major traffic spikes. The site needed to integrate video and tracking tools without compromising speed or user experience.

Administration

An intuitive admin panel for quick updates during broadcasts.

Concurrent users

Infrastructure prepared for thousands of users joining at once during 15-second voting windows.

Video

Website optimization to keep video delivery smooth and stable for the audience.

The solution

TikTok-inspired design

We built the site on WordPress with a TikTok-inspired design, making it easy to navigate and familiar to the show's audience.

Cloudflare infrastructure

We configured Cloudflare to keep the site stable and deliver content efficiently during peak traffic.

Live analytics

We integrated Google Analytics to track user behavior on the site during live events.
