The starting point

The products had value.
The brand needed to show it.

Based in Tlalnepantla, Mat Depot distributes technical rubber and PVC mats and flooring for industry, kitchens, gyms and commercial spaces. It had products, inventory and customers; it needed an identity and digital experience that matched its offering.

B2B buyers aren't simply looking for 'a mat.' They need to understand material, thickness, application and quote requirements. The challenge was to turn that information into a clear experience and a recognizable brand.

The strategy: align what the brand communicates, what the website enables and what the sales team can follow up on.

02 / Brand strategy

Materials built to last.
An identity built to be recognized.

The rebrand started with qualities inherent in the product: strength, density and precision. The Factory Mono system turns that character into a restrained, technical identity.

We redesigned the logo around a block 'M,' a monochrome palette and a cleaner composition. The brand now has a visual language that works across the website, social media, catalog and printed materials.

More than a new logo: a definition of how the brand behaves.

The 30-section brandbook covers essence, voice, logo, color, typography, photography, applications and usage rules. Deliverables included seven vector logo versions, color references, design variables and templates.

The voice was designed for technical buyers: specific facts, direct language and recommendations by application. Its purpose is to help people decide without relying on superlatives.

05 / SEO & GEO

Visibility starts
with clarity.

The search visibility work connected site architecture, product information and editorial content to answer real buying questions.

SEO: a navigable structure

  • Accessible HTML pages and individual product URLs.
  • Titles, descriptions, headings and internal links.
  • Sitemap, canonicals and redirects from previous URLs.
  • Structured data for the business, products and content.
  • Search Console to monitor queries, pages and clicks.

Between October 13 and November 8, 2026, Search Console recorded buying-intent queries: 'tapete para trabajar de pie' (standing-work mat) appeared near position 9 and 'tapete antiderrapante' (non-slip mat) around position 23.

In daily monitoring from November 13–16, Mat Depot reached first place for 'tapetes de hule mayoreo Edomex' (wholesale rubber mats in the State of Mexico) on several days. Queries such as 'mejor piso para gimnasio' (best gym flooring) and 'tapete antifatiga industrial CDMX' (industrial anti-fatigue mat in Mexico City) entered the top five on specific dates.

GEO: useful information for AI answers

  • Content organized by application and buying need.
  • Tables, FAQs and clear descriptions.
  • Consistent business and contact details.
  • Accessible, linked content that can be discovered.
  • Exploratory monitoring of mentions and referral traffic from assistants.

Between October 13 and November 9, 2026, some website traffic was already coming from ChatGPT, according to GA4. Daily monitoring shows intermittent appearances by Mat Depot in ChatGPT and Google's AI.

The foundation is the same: useful, accessible and consistent information.

07 / CRM & measurement

From first contact
to follow-up.

Sales operations were managed in Excel. A CRM was implemented and contact database organization began, work that is still in progress.

Alongside this, Google Analytics 4 and Search Console were added to monitor visits, organic performance and contact actions. Form submissions and WhatsApp clicks are measured separately and with consent.

A duplicate form event on the thank-you page was corrected, and content-sharing actions were separated from actions that contact sales.

Website sessions and users show early progress: +69% and +100% comparing November 9–15 with November 2–8, 2026. Organic Google sessions grew +44%. Contacts remain an early signal: the next step is to connect each WhatsApp click and form submission with the sales record.

Questions about the project

Clear scope. Clear results.

What changed in the buying experience?

The project organized the catalog into individual product pages and added filters, comparison tools and contact options, helping buyers arrive at the quote stage better informed.

Do the published percentages mean more sales?

No. They measure changes in visibility and sessions. The scope details appear at the end of this page.

How does Mood integrate AI into its services?

AI supports analysis, production and automation within each service's scope, with team review. Its application is defined around the specific problem. This doesn't imply Mat Depot has a live AI assistant or guarantee search recommendations.
