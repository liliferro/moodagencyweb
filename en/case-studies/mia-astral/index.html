Background

A distinctive voice.
Enormous potential.

When Mood began working with miastral in 2013, the brand already had its most important asset: Mia's expertise and an audience that connected with her approach to astrology as a tool for personal growth.

The next step was to turn that connection into a more complete digital experience: accessible content, a structured offer, owned communication channels and operations capable of supporting new products and markets.

The initial design of the membership area, now SECCIÓN PARA MIEMBROS (Members' Section), documented in November 2013, marks one of the first milestones in a relationship that continues into 2026.

The challenge

Growth across every part of the business.

Turn attention into a relationship

Connect the social community with the website, exclusive content and products, giving people reasons to return.

Make access and purchasing easier

Support the evolution of the membership and store, improving the journey from discovery and choice to purchase and enjoying the content.

Coordinate an expanding brand

Bring campaigns, launches, support, content and measurement together while preserving Mia's voice.

The solution

An ecosystem connecting every part of the business.

As a marketing agency specializing in technology, measurement and AI, Mood connects the brand's goals with its channels, digital experience and understanding of its audience.

Community & discovery

Content, social media, campaigns and search visibility introduce Mia's work to new audiences. The website's free tools create more ways to discover the brand.

02 · CONVERT

Website, membership & products

The platform connects free content, SECCIÓN PARA MIEMBROS (Members' Section), classes and the store. Work on messaging and the buying experience helps turn interest into a customer relationship.

Relationships & continuity

Website & experience

A platform to discover, learn and return to.

The miastral website connects free content, a birth chart calculator, transits, classes, membership and products. Each element serves a different need and creates another opportunity to connect with the community.

Mood has supported the evolution of these journeys through marketing, analysis, coordination and digital experience improvements, alongside the miastral team and the technology specialists involved at each stage.

Tools that bring people closer to the brand

The free birth chart connects a personal search with a useful website experience. Its development adds a channel for acquisition and discovery to the ecosystem.

Measuring the experience

In 2023, the membership page's conversion rate saw a 44.7% relative improvement compared with 2022. Mobile conversion grew 143% and the subscription page's loading time fell 26%.

Results for the period shown; they do not describe every page's performance or represent a current 2026 measurement.

Digital development & acquisition

The birth chart: free value that attracts new audiences.

As the ecosystem evolved, Mood supported the development of the free birth chart section: an experience that lets people generate their chart on the website and take their first steps into the world of miastral.

Its role as a lead magnet connects a specific audience need with a useful, personalized experience. The brand offers value from the first interaction and opens a path to its content, classes, products and membership.

The section turns the brand's expertise into a free digital tool, available to support discovery and strengthen the relationship with the community.

Community, owned channels & data

From reach to lasting relationships.

A community expanding its reach

The approximate 3,389% growth on Instagram between December 2014 and November 2026 reflects the expansion of Mia's main account during the collaboration. The strategy connects that presence with content, tools and products.

Growth extends beyond one network: combined followers across the social accounts compared grew 1,659% during the collaboration and the YouTube channel grew 1,250% from 2015, measured through 2026.

Contact beyond social media

Email marketing, acquisition workflows and customer support help guide the audience's journey. Conversation and email automations add touchpoints and follow-up opportunities.

Email stands out for its audience response: its key-event rate per user was approximately 349% higher than organic search during the measured period. An owned channel that strengthens community relationships.

A community that stays

At the end of 2023, membership retention reached 87%. Between 2024 and 2025, renewal value rose 19.7% and unique female customers grew 14.4%.

These are signs of a relationship combining continuity, value and a growing customer base, reflecting the shared work of Mia, her team and Mood.

Learning that builds over time

Historical analysis, campaign measurement and store monitoring help identify opportunities. Mood connects data interpretation with decisions about communication, experience and operations.

AI in everyday work

More capacity for the team. Mia's voice at the center.

Mood has supported the adoption of AI across miastral's tools and processes, bringing it into different areas of the team to streamline tasks, organize work and respond to community needs.

Productivity in everyday tasks

AI helps organize audio and materials, prepare briefs, review work and handle operational tasks. Mood adapts these tools to the brand's processes to simplify steps and make the team's work easier.

More accessible knowledge

Guides, reference resources and reusable tools help organize brand information and establish shared criteria. The team can find and make better use of existing knowledge in their daily work.

Faster, more consistent support

AI helps organize FAQs and draft answers based on validated product and service information. The team reviews each answer to better support people and their needs.

The voice, ideas and content remain Mia's

Mia creates and decides what she wants to share. AI serves as operational support: it does not invent teachings or alter her thinking, words or interpretations. Work starts from her original materials, with editorial judgment and human validation at its core.

Mood connects technology and processes so the team can use AI day to day, with a clear purpose: spend more of their time on content quality and the community experience.

Mood's contribution · Marketing, technology, measurement & AI

Technology connects the content. Measurement helps us understand the people.

Mia creates the content. Mood connects strategy, technology and audience understanding to help it reach the people who need it.

We are a marketing agency specializing in technology, measurement and AI. At miastral, that means ongoing work to make content easier to discover, improve the digital experience and learn from the community's response to guide the next decisions.

01 · CONNECT

Technology that serves the experience

We combine plugins, integrations and custom development to adapt the ecosystem to the brand and its audience. Each solution has a purpose: simplify access, organize content, connect journeys and make the experience more useful.

The free birth chart section is one example: it turns an interest in astrology into a personalized experience and an entry point into Mia's world.

02 · UNDERSTAND

Measure to understand what works

We analyze search, navigation, engagement and conversion signals alongside the community's questions and needs. This helps us identify what sparks interest, which content gets a response and where people encounter difficulties.

That learning informs decisions about topics, formats, messages, channels and usability. Data adds context to the team's judgment and helps prioritize improvements.

03 · REACH

SEO to reach the right audience

We work on search visibility and information organization to connect Mia's content with the questions people are already asking.

The goal is to attract an audience with a real interest and help them find a useful answer, discover related content and move toward the experience that best fits their needs.

Between November 2020 and October 2023, clicks from Google grew 2,780%. The free birth chart connects the brand with searches such as 'cómo saber mi carta astral' (how to find my birth chart), turning a specific need into an entry point to the ecosystem.

Between May 2023 and November 2026, more than 99.9% of visits were not attributed to paid media channels. This includes visits whose source could not be identified; it is not a measure of exclusively organic traffic.

Listen, adjust and keep learning

We connect what the audience searches for, reads and asks with marketing decisions and ecosystem improvements. An ongoing cycle of observation, adjustment and evaluation helps us respond to their needs every day.

The continuity of the relationship lets us build knowledge of the brand and its community, and apply it to new content, products and communication moments.

Mia brings her expertise and editorial vision. Mood connects strategy, technology and audience learning in collaboration with the internal team and ecosystem specialists.
