Nawis App

Mobile app development and branding: connecting physical creativity with the digital world.

Background

Nawis is the first Venezuelan brand of wooden toys designed for customization. They are three-dimensional blank canvases ready for color—little intergalactic friends that spark creativity here on Earth.

The challenge

The physical product presented a challenge: user anxiety. You only get one chance to paint your wooden toy. Make a mistake, and there's no undo button.

Fear of mistakes

How could we remove the fear of getting it wrong and encourage creativity before users touched the real toy?

A digital bridge

We needed a bridge between the physical toy and the digital environments where users spend their time.

Community

Encourage users to share their creations and inspire others.

The solution

A gamified app

We designed a gamified mobile app that lets users color and customize their Nawis digitally as many times as they like before painting the real thing.

Room to experiment

Intuitive tools—brush, eyedropper and eraser—let users try colors and designs without risk, easing blank-canvas anxiety.

A social gallery

Native social integration made it easy to share digital designs, encouraging sharing and building a community inspiration gallery.
