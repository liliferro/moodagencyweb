01 / The client’s challenge

An established business. An opportunity to connect its digital operations.

PHASA is a Mexican company specializing in rubber products and materials for industrial applications. It already had a technical catalog, active campaigns, an experienced sales team and HubSpot in use. Its next step was to make better use of those resources.

The client identified three related needs: gain visibility on Google and in AI-generated answers; use artificial intelligence to reduce manual work; and make stronger use of its CRM to follow through on sales opportunities.

Search visibility to strengthen

The audit found organic demand and pages already appearing in search, alongside opportunities to improve click-through, organize content around buying intent and explain product applications more clearly.

Connect acquisition with sales

Measurement combined actions of different business value. Visits and clicks needed to be distinguished from valid leads, and acquisition needed to be connected with deal progress in HubSpot.

AI applied to daily work

Marketing and sales needed practical AI skills for their work: producing technical content, analyzing information, preparing follow-ups and keeping the CRM up to date.

02 / Mood’s approach

Develop the technology and team adoption together.

We worked on two connected fronts: a B2B strategy to attract the right buyers and a sales operation able to follow up. We identified buyer personas, needs, objections and decision criteria, then defined the funnel, messaging, campaigns and media plan with AI support. In parallel, we trained marketing and sales to apply the strategy and manage opportunities in HubSpot.

Diagnosis and buyer personas

We used AI to analyze search, analytics, campaigns and the CRM, identifying demand, buyer profiles and friction between acquisition and sales follow-up.

Strategy, funnel and campaigns

We delivered the strategy, conversion funnel, SEO/GEO architecture and media plan, with audiences, messages and content for each stage. ChatGPT and Claude supported analysis, planning and production, with team review.

Training and automation

We trained marketing and sales and implemented follow-up processes, automation and pipeline reports. Each opportunity was linked to a stage, an owner and a next action.

The result was a connected sales journey: attract buyers, help them choose and follow through on opportunities using data and AI.

03 / The solution: search visibility

Turn technical expertise into content that helps buyers find and choose PHASA.

We started with an audit of Google Analytics, Search Console and Google Ads. Reviewing them together helped identify categories with demand and focus the strategy on industrial buying questions: which material to choose, what application it serves and which specifications matter.

SEO: address search intent

We organized the SEO strategy around categories, products and applications. We delivered priority pages, technical guides, titles, descriptions and internal links to support buyers from an initial need to a quote request. AI supported research and content development, with technical review.

GEO: make the information easier for AI to understand

We incorporated GEO principles into the content: direct explanations, FAQs, material comparisons and structured data. Technical information was organized to make it easier for both buyers and AI answer engines to understand.

The editorial delivery included a home page, a pilot page, six category pages and five guides, together with FAQs and publishing guidelines. PHASA received a content structure aligned with its products, buyers and sales funnel.

Measurement was designed around business outcomes: distinguish interactions from valid leads and track progress from lead to opportunity and sale.

04 / The solution: marketing team

Train the team to create, analyze and work with AI.

The search strategy needed a team able to sustain it. We delivered training tailored to PHASA, using sessions, exercises and guides to apply AI to content strategy, analysis and recurring tasks.

Provide context before creating

The team worked with a brand brief and defined buyer personas. Using ChatGPT and Claude, it developed messages, content, campaign concepts and calls to action based on industrial buyers’ problems, objections and buying stages.

Build repeatable processes

With Claude and Cowork, the team learned to organize documents, create reusable instructions—skills—and connect tools. Practice covered audits, data queries, campaign planning and file management.

Analyze and review critically

Sessions demonstrated analytics queries and behavior analysis with Clarity. Training included human review, technical validation and access permissions for using AI with work information.

Training established a repeatable method: brand context → buyer persona → funnel stage → content, campaign or analysis → review → next action. The team received materials and processes to plan marketing, develop campaigns and maintain the media plan with AI.

05 / The solution: sales team

HubSpot, Breeze, ChatGPT and Claude applied to the sales pipeline.

PHASA was already using HubSpot. The challenge was to make better use of it in daily follow-up: maintain useful stages and dates, record conversation context and ensure every opportunity has an owner and a next action.

We organized sales work in HubSpot and trained salespeople and managers to use Breeze—HubSpot’s AI—alongside ChatGPT and Claude. The team learned to query the CRM, summarize customer histories, prepare follow-ups, review stalled deals and keep the pipeline updated through authorized access and reviewed changes.

From contact to close: a shared process

  1. 01

    Contact

    Find and record the customer without duplicating information.

  2. 02

    Opportunity

    Create the deal and assign an owner.

  3. 03

    Quote

    Record the value, reference and a realistic close date.

  4. 04

    Follow-up

    Schedule the next task and record the context.

  5. 05

    Close

    Update the outcome and record the loss reason when applicable.

AI for salespeople

An AI-assisted follow-up process to prioritize tasks, find inactive quotes, summarize history before a call and prepare messages with context. Sales notes were turned into useful CRM records.

AI for managers

Automated reports and AI-assisted pipeline reviews to identify stalled opportunities, analyze loss reasons and define actions for each salesperson using HubSpot data.

Trained team

Training included guides, exercises and work on real sales processes. The team adopted a shared routine for reviewing information, validating changes and recording the next action for each deal.

06 / Process automation

Automated follow-up and reporting, with clear ownership.

The implementation connected pipeline management with follow-up tasks, alerts and automated reports. AI supported opportunity analysis and preparation of next actions, while the sales team retained control of decisions and customer relationships.

Implemented automations

  • Follow-up tasks linked to sent quotes.
  • Alerts for inactive opportunities.
  • Identification of overdue close dates for review.
  • Automated pipeline reports for sales reviews.

AI-assisted sales follow-up

Breeze, ChatGPT and Claude supported customer-history reviews, deal analysis and follow-up drafting. Sales owners had organized information to decide which opportunity to address, which context to retrieve and which action to record in HubSpot.

PHASA completed the project with defined sales processes, implemented automation and teams trained to use them. Marketing and sales shared a common working method: buyer context, CRM records, next actions and AI-assisted performance reviews.

07 / Project results

Connected marketing and sales. AI-enabled operations in place.

The project concluded with a defined B2B strategy, buyer-focused SEO/GEO content and organized sales operations in HubSpot. The result was a shared way of working across marketing and sales, supported by training, automation, reporting and AI-assisted follow-up.

Marketing aligned with sales

Identified buyer personas, a defined funnel, SEO/GEO strategy, campaigns and a media plan developed with AI. Content and messaging were aligned with industrial buyers’ needs and decision stages.

Two teams trained in AI

Marketing incorporated ChatGPT and Claude into planning, content and analysis. Sales incorporated Breeze, ChatGPT and Claude into HubSpot workflows, pipeline reviews and opportunity follow-up.

Automated sales management

An organized pipeline, follow-up tasks and alerts, automated reports and AI-assisted analysis. The team gained a shared view of opportunities and the actions needed to address them.
