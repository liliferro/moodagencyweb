01 / The client’s challenge

An established business. An opportunity to connect its digital operations.

PHASA is a Mexican company specializing in rubber products and materials for industrial applications. It already had a technical catalog, active campaigns, an experienced sales team and HubSpot in use. Its next step was to make better use of those resources.

The client identified three related needs: gain visibility on Google and in AI-generated answers; use artificial intelligence to reduce manual work; and make stronger use of its CRM to follow through on sales opportunities.

Search visibility to strengthen

The audit found organic demand and pages already appearing in search, alongside opportunities to improve click-through, organize content around buying intent and explain product applications more clearly.

Connect acquisition with sales

Measurement combined actions of different business value. Visits and clicks needed to be distinguished from valid leads, and acquisition needed to be connected with deal progress in HubSpot.

AI applied to daily work

Marketing and sales needed practical AI skills for their work: producing technical content, analyzing information, preparing follow-ups and keeping the CRM up to date.

02 / Mood’s approach

Develop the technology and team adoption together.

We proposed two parallel workstreams: improve the digital foundation of PHASA, and train the people who produce content, respond to prospects and manage opportunities. Search visibility and automation would then be supported by a clear sales process and a team able to use it.

Diagnose

Review search performance, analytics, campaigns and the CRM to understand demand, measurement and the obstacles to consistent follow-up.

Design and train

Define SEO/GEO architecture and working processes; teach the team to apply AI using brand context, technical information and sales data.

Automate by priority

Identify repetitive tasks and establish rules, owners and controls before integrating channels or activating automated workflows.

The strategy needed to cover the full journey: help buyers find PHASA, understand the solution and receive clear sales follow-up.

03 / The solution: search visibility

Turn technical expertise into content that helps buyers find and choose PHASA.

We started with an audit of Google Analytics, Search Console and Google Ads. Reviewing them together helped identify categories with demand and focus the strategy on industrial buying questions: which material to choose, what application it serves and which specifications matter.

SEO: address search intent

We designed an architecture for PHASA organized by categories, products and applications. Priority pages, technical guides, titles, descriptions and an internal linking plan were prepared to help buyers move from a need to a quote request.

GEO: make the information easier for AI to understand

The strategy also addresses how information is presented to AI answer engines: direct explanations, frequently asked questions, material comparisons and structured data. The aim is to help those tools interpret the technical content and use it as a reference.

The prepared editorial work includes a home page, a pilot page, six category pages and five guides, together with FAQs and publishing guidelines. Implementation is organized in stages, with the client validating technical information.

Measurement was designed around business outcomes: distinguish interactions from valid leads and track progress from lead to opportunity and sale.

04 / The solution: marketing team

Train the team to create, analyze and work with AI.

The search strategy needed a team able to sustain it. We delivered training tailored to PHASA, using sessions, exercises and guides to apply AI to content strategy, analysis and recurring tasks.

Provide context before creating

Training covered brand briefs, buyer profiles, problems, objections and buying stages. This foundation guided messages, content and calls to action using ChatGPT and Claude.

Build repeatable processes

With Claude and Cowork, the team learned to organize documents, create reusable instructions—skills—and connect tools. Practice covered audits, data queries, campaign planning and file management.

Analyze and review critically

Sessions demonstrated analytics queries and behavior analysis with Clarity. Training included human review, technical validation and access permissions for using AI with work information.

The aim was to leave the team with a repeatable method: brand context → buyer need → content or analysis → review → next action. Supporting materials let participants return to the process after each session.

05 / The solution: sales team

Use HubSpot and Breeze to turn customer history into next actions.

PHASA was already using HubSpot. The challenge was to make better use of it in daily follow-up: maintain useful stages and dates, record conversation context and ensure every opportunity has an owner and a next action.

Following the CRM review, sales training focused on the daily work of salespeople and managers. The program covered AI applied to HubSpot, including Breeze—HubSpot’s AI—and Claude or ChatGPT through authorized connectors, depending on the features available in the portal.

From contact to close: a shared process

  1. 01

    Contact

    Find and record the customer without duplicating information.

  2. 02

    Opportunity

    Create the deal and assign an owner.

  3. 03

    Quote

    Record the value, reference and a realistic close date.

  4. 04

    Follow-up

    Schedule the next task and record the context.

  5. 05

    Close

    Update the outcome and record the loss reason when applicable.

AI for salespeople

Exercises for prioritizing tasks, finding quotes without follow-up, summarizing history before a call, preparing messages and turning notes into structured records.

AI for managers

Prompts and a pipeline review routine to identify stalled opportunities, analyze loss reasons and define actions for each salesperson using CRM data.

Controlled practice

Guides, batch-editing exercises and assignments between sessions. Information is queried and reviewed first; changes are validated with the process owner.

06 / Process automation

Define what to automate, which data it needs and who takes over.

Training and CRM discipline form the foundation of the AI Sales Operating System proposed for PHASA: an architecture connecting acquisition, response, quotation, follow-up and analysis. Diagnosis and design help prioritize workflows before they are configured.

Priority automations

  • Create a follow-up task when a quote is sent.
  • Flag opportunities with no recent activity.
  • Identify overdue close dates for review.
  • Prepare a weekly pipeline summary for the manager.

Evolution of the sales system

The design includes AI-assisted WhatsApp response, request qualification, routing to a salesperson, development of the existing quotation tool and reporting dashboards. Each component has a defined role in the sales journey and a staged rollout.

These workflows form part of the implementation proposal. Activation depends on PHASA’s validation, data quality and licensed capabilities. The goal is to reduce repetitive tasks so salespeople can spend more time understanding needs and supporting buying decisions.

07 / Project progress

A shared strategy and teams trained to put it into daily practice.

The work delivered brings together digital diagnosis, SEO/GEO architecture and content, AI training for marketing and sales training with HubSpot. Connecting these workstreams is Mood’s central contribution: defining how to attract relevant demand, work with it and prepare follow-up for automation.

Strategy and content prepared

Channel diagnosis, a search-led commercial architecture, technical content and guidelines for prioritized publishing.

Applied training for two teams

Marketing worked on context, content, analysis and AI-supported processes. Sales worked on follow-up, data and productivity in HubSpot.

Automation roadmap defined

Processes, tasks and integration opportunities identified to progress from training to staged implementation.
