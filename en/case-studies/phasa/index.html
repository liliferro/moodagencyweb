The starting point

Connecting industrial expertise.

PHASA manufactures and markets rubber products for industry and the automotive sector. It presents itself as a specialist since 1984, with a technical catalog, a digital presence and experienced product teams.

The project aims to connect measurement, the answers buyers find and commercial follow-up. For a technical purchase, explaining applications, materials and conditions matters as much as making contact easy.

02 / Brand voice

A shared approach to explaining technical products.

The hule.com.mx voice guide brings together tone, writing rules, a technical glossary and examples. It defines how to explain materials and applications with specific information and clear language that helps buyers compare options.

During marketing training, the team worked on turning brand and customer profiles into AI instructions. The goal is consistent content, supported by human review and technical validation.

03 / Teams at the center

Learning through each team’s work.

Sales

March 30, 2026 class: AI support for organizing ideas, preparing follow-ups and analyzing objections, with the salesperson’s judgment at the center.

A shared foundation

April 24, 2026 masterclass: AI fundamentals, practical tool use and applications in PHASA’s industrial context.

Marketing

Practical sessions starting in April: brand instructions, content production, audits and reports tailored to the team’s needs.

The fourth format is the HubSpot program, which started in November and is described in the sales workstream. Training materials are deliverables; their existence alone does not measure adoption or productivity.

04 / Web architecture

Finding the right information before requesting a quote.

The new hule.com.mx structure has been designed and documented. It organizes categories, products and industry applications, with a proposed linking and redirect plan to support the transition from the existing site.

From category to application

Six pillars: industrial mats; PVC strip curtains; neoprene and rubber rolls; polyurethane; joints and bearings; and industry applications.

Pages that help buyers choose

Technical information, frequently asked questions and links to help buyers understand alternatives before contacting sales.

Publication in stages

The plan starts with a dielectric mat pilot page, followed by the home page, pillars and guides as their content is validated.

As of this case’s reporting date, the architecture and content are prepared. The new structure is not presented as a fully published website.

05 / SEO & GEO

Answering buying questions more effectively.

SEO: structure and content

  • Proposed URLs organized by category and application.
  • Titles and descriptions prepared for each page.
  • Links between pillars, guides and product pages.
  • A redirect map to reduce migration risks.
  • GA4 and Search Console as the basis for monitoring.

GEO: understandable information

  • Content organized around buying needs and applications.
  • 50 frequently asked questions prepared.
  • Guides, tables and clear technical descriptions.
  • Consistent company and contact information.

Visibility and referral traffic from AI assistants will be evaluated as content is published. Content and structured data do not guarantee indexing, rankings or AI recommendations.

06 / Content and production

From a catalog to useful explanations.

The five technical guides address specific decisions: selecting rubber, avoiding mistakes when choosing PVC strip curtains, distinguishing mat types, comparing neoprene, EPDM and nitrile, and understanding specifications for the energy industry.

The work also includes a HeyGen Manual for PHASA, version 1.0 from November 2026, guiding short-video production with AI avatars. It is a production guide, not evidence of a published video campaign.

07 / CRM and sales follow-up

Using existing history to organize the next step.

PHASA uses HubSpot and has an existing sales history. In September 2026, a read-only portal audit was documented. This work informed the sales blueprint, nine proposed improvements and a session plan.

The HubSpot training program began on November 17, 2026. The first session covered productivity with AI and Breeze, supported by a manual: a five-step lead journey, a five-point review, twelve prompts and a practical assignment.

The program includes working sessions and a shared introduction to AI before progressing to more specific tools. Training is ongoing; CRM improvements are implemented subject to PHASA’s approvals.

Questions about the project

Clear scope. Clear results.

What has been delivered and what remains planned?

The audit, prepared architecture and content, blueprint and described training are documented. Full content publication and later automation phases remain planned or subject to validation.

Do the published counts mean more sales?

No. The figures count deliverables and training formats. They are not business growth metrics or a promise of results.

Does PHASA already have an implemented AI agent?

This case does not claim that an AI agent has been launched. It describes training, content and a phased plan for AI and automation applications.
