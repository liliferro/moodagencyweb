Privacy notice

Last updated: November 13, 2026.

Data controller and contact

Iron Mussa Corp., which operates this website under the Mood Agency brand, with its address at Avenida Río San Joaquín 498, Mexico City, Mexico, is responsible for processing personal data collected through mood-agency.com. For privacy inquiries, email info@mood-agency.com.

Data we collect

When contacting us, you may provide your first and last name, email address, phone number and project information. Avoid including sensitive personal data or third-party information that is unnecessary for your inquiry. Technical providers may process IP addresses, browser data and logs needed to deliver and protect the service.

How we use it

We use contact information to respond to inquiries, understand needs, prepare proposals and follow up on a potential business relationship. Accepting optional cookies is not a condition for handling your request. The contact form does not subscribe you to a newsletter.

We use analytics to understand visits and interactions only with your optional consent. Advertising and personalization require separate consent. You can decline these purposes or withdraw your choice in 'Cookie settings.'

Providers and disclosures

The site is hosted on Vercel, the domain is managed through Cloudflare and the form uses HubSpot. Measurement uses Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity; Meta advertising tags are enabled only with advertising consent. Clarity analyzes interactions and is configured to mask the form area. These providers may process information outside Mexico to deliver their services. If you contact us via WhatsApp, that provider's terms and policies also apply.

We do not sell data provided through the form. Any transfer requiring consent will be subject to applicable law. We may disclose information when legally required or in response to a valid request from an authority.

Cookies and preferences

Your browser stores your cookie choice for up to 180 days. Optional tags remain blocked until you accept the relevant category. If you consent to analytics, we may retain campaign parameters for the session to associate an inquiry with its source. Advertising identifiers require advertising consent.

You can change your preferences in the footer and clear storage through your browser. Rejecting cookies does not prevent you from browsing the site or contacting us via form, email or phone.

ARCO rights, withdrawal and restrictions

You may request access, rectification, cancellation or objection to the processing of your data, withdraw consent where applicable or restrict its use and disclosure. Send your request to info@mood-agency.com with your name, a way to respond, a description of the right or request and details that help locate your information. For rectification, specify the correction requested.

We will explain how to verify your identity or authority to act; do not send identity documents through the business inquiry form. We will communicate our determination within applicable statutory periods: up to twenty days from receipt of the request and, if applicable, implement it within the following fifteen days, with justified extensions as permitted by law. Cancellation may be subject to legal retention obligations.

Retention and protection

We retain information for as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. You may request cancellation when the data is no longer necessary. Access should be limited to those involved in handling inquiries and operating the service. No internet transmission is entirely risk-free.

Changes to this notice

We will publish changes on this page with an updated date. For questions about the notice or exercising your rights, use the email address above.
