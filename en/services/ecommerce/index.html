Services

Ecommerce & sales with AI

We connect AI, ecommerce and automation to improve the buying journey and customer relationships. We use AI to analyze behavior and friction, design your store or membership platform and prioritize content, segmentation and follow-up based on available data and integrations.

The Mood approach

How we use AI and technology

  • AI supports catalog organization and copywriting; your business validates prices, features and terms.
  • We analyze purchasing and repeat-customer patterns to propose segments and retention actions.
  • We integrate cart follow-up, communications and recommendation features where the platform and data support them.

How we assess its contribution

We measure conversion, order value and repeat purchases. For memberships, we also track cancellations and retention when data is available.

What we can deliver

  • A store or membership platform with compatible payments and systems
  • AI-assisted purchase and catalog analysis
  • Segmentation and follow-up workflows based on available data
  • A conversion and retention plan with defined metrics

The final scope is agreed in each project proposal.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with memberships?

Yes. The Mia Astral case study describes our work on a membership platform and an ecosystem of products and content.

How much does a project cost?

The investment depends on the scope, integrations, content and level of support required. We prepare a proposal after understanding your goals; there is no single price that fits every project.

How long does a project take?

The timeline depends on deliverables and dependencies, including content, access, reviews and integrations. We agree on it in writing before work begins.

What happens after I get in touch?

We review your message, contact you to clarify your goals and assess the scope. If we're a good fit, we prepare a proposal with deliverables, a timeline and next steps.

Do you guarantee sales or Google rankings?

No. We set goals and measure progress, but results depend on your market, investment, offer and other factors beyond the agency's control.
