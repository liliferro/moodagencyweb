Services

Performance marketing & lead generation with AI

We combine advertising, AI and sales technology to generate and develop opportunities. We use AI to analyze campaigns and create messaging variations under review. We connect ads, landing pages and CRM to assess lead quality and guide optimization.

The Mood approach

How we use AI and technology

  • AI helps identify performance patterns and propose hypotheses we test against campaign data.
  • We generate and review creative variations to test messages, value propositions and landing pages.
  • We design AI-assisted inquiry classification and summaries to help prioritize sales work; rules and follow-up are agreed with sales.

How we assess its contribution

We evaluate cost per opportunity, lead quality and sales progress when the CRM contains enough information.

What we can deliver

  • Campaign planning and funnel analysis with AI support
  • Ad and landing page variations for testing
  • Conversion measurement and CRM integration where feasible
  • AI-assisted qualification and follow-up within available data and scope

The final scope is agreed in each project proposal.

Frequently asked questions

Is ad spend included?

Media budget and agency fees are itemized separately in the proposal. We do not activate ad spend without agreeing it with you.

How much does a project cost?

The investment depends on the scope, integrations, content and level of support required. We prepare a proposal after understanding your goals; there is no single price that fits every project.

How long does a project take?

The timeline depends on deliverables and dependencies, including content, access, reviews and integrations. We agree on it in writing before work begins.

What happens after I get in touch?

We review your message, contact you to clarify your goals and assess the scope. If we're a good fit, we prepare a proposal with deliverables, a timeline and next steps.

Do you guarantee sales or Google rankings?

No. We set goals and measure progress, but results depend on your market, investment, offer and other factors beyond the agency's control.
