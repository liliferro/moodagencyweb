What is GEO?

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization: work focused on how a brand and its information may appear in AI-generated answers. It combines understandable content, evidence, technical accessibility and observation of responses.

At Mood, we connect it with SEO and the sales journey. A mention can create a discovery opportunity; your website must then help the buyer understand your offer and get in touch.

Win industrial customers and enter new markets

How can an industrial company find customers beyond referrals and trade shows?

Start by defining which companies can buy, what problem you solve and which locations you can serve. Your website should explain applications, capabilities and purchasing criteria, with a clear path to request a quote. At Mood, we build that foundation to capture demand from search engines and AI answers, combining it with other channels when scope calls for it. Content complements your sales work and relationships that already perform.

How can we attract B2B buyers looking for solutions who don't yet know our brand?

Organize content around problems and applications as well as product names. A manufacturer or distributor can explain how to choose a solution, which operating conditions to assess and what details are needed for a quote. Mood connects those answers with your commercial pages. You don't need to know the term GEO to engage us: we start with how a new buyer discovers you.

How can we find distributors or wholesale customers for our products?

Distinguish distributors from end buyers. A partner page should explain available product lines, coverage, the distributor profile, support and collaboration requirements you can disclose. The form should identify the company, location and sales channel. Mood can work on visibility and the contact journey; distributor approval and commercial terms remain with your team.

Can we attract industrial customers in other Mexican cities?

We can plan a regional strategy where you genuinely have supply or service capacity. We first document coverage, logistics, installation and support, as applicable. We create regional pages only when they offer distinct, useful information; repeating the same text with a different city doesn't help buyers. The assessment determines which markets and pages to prioritize.

Turn visits into quote requests sales can use

Why does our website get visits but very few quote requests?

There may be several issues: poorly matched visitors, an unclear offer, missing technical information or friction when contacting you. Total traffic alone cannot identify the cause. We review search queries, entry pages, calls to action and available inquiries to prioritize changes. If measurement isn't configured, we first define how to record those steps.

How can we attract volume buyers and reduce consumer inquiries?

Be explicit about whom you serve: industries, applications, coverage and minimum orders, where applicable. Content and forms can ask for company, intended use, approximate volume and location. This helps guide and evaluate inquiries but won't eliminate every poor-fit contact. Mood agrees with sales on the conditions an opportunity must meet before reporting it as qualified.

What information does sales need to qualify an industrial lead?

As a starting point: an identifiable company, a specific need, product or application, a serviceable location, project scale and estimated timing. It also helps to understand the contact's role in the purchase and agree on a next step. Criteria are adapted to your business. A corporate email or catalog download alone doesn't establish buying intent or budget.

How can content help when sales take months and involve procurement, engineering and leadership?

Each participant needs different information: engineering assesses technical suitability, procurement compares supply terms and leadership reviews impact and risk. We propose content your contact can share internally, such as selection guides, verified data sheets and documented cases. Sales can use it in follow-up. Its contribution is evaluated alongside opportunity progress, without attributing an entire sale to a single read.

Get considered in Google and AI answers

How can buyers find us when searching for suppliers on Google or ChatGPT?

Publish clear information about what you supply, applications, coverage and demonstrable capabilities on accessible, interconnected pages. Mood reviews that foundation, organizes buyer questions and observes when the brand appears or its website is cited. Repeating keywords isn't enough, and we can't guarantee recommendations. The work aims to make useful, verifiable information available for evaluating your business.

Why do our competitors appear in AI answers while we don't?

One answer can't reveal the cause. We first repeat a defined sample of questions and record the product, date, context and cited sources. Then we review differences in public information: offer clarity, accessible pages, question coverage and relevant references. This supports prioritization; it doesn't mean we know the algorithm or that copying a competitor will produce the same result.

Is uploading our PDF catalog enough for buyers to find us?

A catalog can support evaluation, but your website should also explain products, applications and selection criteria on browsable pages. That lets buyers reach a specific answer and contact you without going through the entire document. Mood assesses which catalog information deserves a page and retains downloadable data sheets as supporting material, with specifications validated by your team.

What kind of agency can help generate B2B opportunities if we already have marketing and sales teams?

Look for a team that understands your technical offer, agrees on qualification criteria with sales and connects content, visibility and measurement with business goals. Mood works with B2B companies that have a website and follow-up capacity. GEO scope covers visibility and content; CRM, automation, sales training and campaigns are defined separately when needed. Share your website and the customers you want to attract so we can assess the fit.

GEO service scope and delivery

What is GEO, and how is it different from SEO?

GEO, or Generative Engine Optimization, focuses on improving how a business may be discovered and represented in AI-generated answers. SEO helps search engines find and understand its pages. Both rely on useful content, technical access and evidence; GEO adds observation of answers, mentions and citations.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No. Answers change with the question, context, product and timing. We commit to agreed scope, deliverables and measurement—not a fixed ranking or guaranteed recommendation.

Do you offer GEO for industrial manufacturers and distributors?

Yes. We start with the applications, products, coverage, capabilities and criteria their buyers use to evaluate suppliers. We prioritize commercial and technical questions the company can answer with verifiable information.

Which AI tools can we monitor for brand visibility?

We can define a question sample for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI experiences, depending on available access and agreed scope. We document the product or API, date and context; one observation doesn't represent what all users see.

Do I need to rebuild my website?

Not necessarily. We first check whether content is accessible, whether it explains your offer clearly and which pages are missing. The proposal may prioritize changes to existing pages, new content or specific technical improvements.

How long does the service take, and what does it cost?

It depends on the site's condition, market, available pages and sources, and the work required. After reviewing your goal, we define scope, investment, timing and responsibilities in writing. There is no guaranteed date for appearing in AI answers.

How will you measure its contribution to the business?

We separate indexing and searches, observed mentions and citations, referred visits and received inquiries. Where CRM and measurement data allow, we review sales opportunities. A mention is not presented as an attributed sale.

Will you publish AI content without review?

Not as part of this approach. AI can support research and drafts; claims, specifications and brand information need human review and sources. We agree the approval process with your team.
